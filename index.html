The "Use my location" button searches within a radius you choose (5 to 45 miles) of your device's location, only after you allow it. Your position is rounded to about 7 miles before it is used. The small map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles, which see the rounded area (not your exact position). If device location isn't available, it falls back to an approximate lookup from your IP address using geojs.io. Nothing is stored.
